Product overview

A live audiovisual instrument for building video collages, generative graphics and typographic scenes that move with music.

What it does

Live footage collage

Combine up to four independent video decks. Control playback, segments, loops, frame hold and deck locks while building freeform compositions.

Generative layers and overlays

Add geometry, waveform and GPU particle generators, Text Art, still images, animated images and video overlays. Adjust transforms, opacity, blend modes and layer effects.

Typography as image

Create animated text, styled runs, vertical writing, path-based layouts and English/Japanese text material.

Sound and musical control

Use audio input or system loopback, six normalized Performance Macros, LFOs, envelopes, step sequences, MIDI and OSC from Sonic Pi.

Scenes and projection

Capture and recall VJ scenes, schedule events by bar and beat, and output to a fullscreen projector display while keeping a local preview.

Three workspaces

  • PREPARE: organize footage and browse assets.
  • COMPOSE: build the visual composition.
  • PERF: operate the image live with music and external controls.

Who it is for

  • VJs and live visual performers — the clearest fit, given the four decks, composition layers, scenes, macros and projector output.
  • Electronic musicians / Sonic Pi users — useful for building visual performances synchronized through OSC.
  • Audiovisual and media artists — combine recorded footage with procedural images and typographic material.
  • Generative artists and creative coders — perform visual systems rather than render one fixed output.
  • Typography-led artists — work with bilingual text, vertical writing and animated type.

What it is not

AV Translator is not a conventional non-linear video editor, a DAW, a full 3D authoring suite or a finished-master recorder. Source media and Sonic Pi are installed separately.
