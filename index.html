PLATE 01THE INSTRUMENT

NOT A TIMELINE EDITOR

One composition.
Many possible states.

Bring footage, text, procedural graphics and visual effects into one composition. Shape it with sound, rhythm, MIDI or Sonic Pi, then send it to a screen or projector.

Build a visual system, then operate it as part of a performance. The image is not only rendered once; it can keep changing while music is happening.

PLATE 04INTENDED USE

WHO WORKS WITH IT

For people who
perform with images.

VJs and live visual performers are the clearest fit. It also suits electronic musicians working with Sonic Pi, audiovisual artists, generative artists and typography-led performers.

It is not a replacement for a conventional video editor, a DAW or a 3D authoring suite. It is a live composition and performance environment.
